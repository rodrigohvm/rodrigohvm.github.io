Project 1 · in production

Management system for a small lodge

Vila Pinheiro Cabanas is a four-cabin lodge in Santa Teresa, Brazil, which I co-founded. Guests were served over WhatsApp, and the records were spread across spreadsheets and separate tools. Today a single system answers guests, keeps the bookings, alerts the team and issues the tax invoices.

Claude Code and Codex

What the system does

  • Answers guests on WhatsApp and Instagram Direct through the official Meta API: triage, price list, booking flow, cabin access codes and installment reminders.
  • One CRM brings together the dashboard, conversations, bookings, customers, campaigns, calendar, operations, finance and tax.
  • Collects Pix instant payments and confirms them automatically with the bank.
  • Issues electronic service invoices (NFS-e) through the national API. The first invoice was authorized on 12 Nov 2026.
  • Includes guest digital check-in, cleaning rosters and a daily backup with tested restore.

The decision I would highlight

The guest-facing bot does not use AI. It did at first, and in August 2026 I removed it. Text rules handle the forms at zero cost per message and never make up an answer for a guest. AI stayed where it pays off most: writing the code, auditing it and producing monthly reports.

How quality was ensured

  • Three test suites on every change: 3,575 unit tests, 60 screen tests and 260 end-to-end tests. They run on synthetic data, with no real messages, charges or invoices.
  • Four audits between 29 Oct and 21 Nov 2026, one of them external. In the 12 Nov review, 18 families of defects were reproduced and fixed.
  • 150 numbered, dated product decisions, with a rule that none is reversed without asking.
  • One release per day, batched, after review.
  • I led the company's approval as a Meta Tech Provider and moved the business number to the API without interrupting service.
